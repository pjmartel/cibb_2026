Prediction and structural comparison

  • AlphaFold 2 and confidence: Jumper, J., Evans, R., Pritzel, A., et al. (2021). Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold. Nature 596(7873), 583–589. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2.

  • AlphaFold 3 and biomolecular complexes: Abramson, J., Adler, J., Dunger, J., et al. (2024). Accurate structure prediction of biomolecular interactions with AlphaFold 3. Nature 630(8016), 493–500. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-07487-w.

  • Boltz-2: structure and affinity: Passaro, S., Corso, G., Wohlwend, J., et al. (2025). Boltz-2: Towards Accurate and Efficient Binding Affinity Prediction. bioRxiv, 2025.06.14.659707 [preprint]. DOI: 10.1101/2025.06.14.659707.

  • Foldseek structure search: van Kempen, M., Kim, S. S., Tumescheit, C., et al. (2024). Fast and accurate protein structure search with Foldseek. Nature Biotechnology 42(2), 243–246. DOI: 10.1038/s41587-023-01773-0.

  • TM-align structural alignment: Zhang, Y., and Skolnick, J. (2005). TM-align: a protein structure alignment algorithm based on the TM-score. Nucleic Acids Research 33(7), 2302–2309. DOI: 10.1093/nar/gki524.

  • T4 lysozyme–benzene: PDB 4W52: Merski, M., Fischer, M., Balius, T. E., Eidam, O., and Shoichet, B. K. (2015). Homologous ligands accommodated by discrete conformations of a buried cavity. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences USA 112(16), 5039–5044. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1500806112.

Sequence models, ensembles and design

  • EVcouplings framework: Hopf, T. A., Green, A. G., Schubert, B., et al. (2019). The EVcouplings Python framework for coevolutionary sequence analysis. Bioinformatics 35(9), 1582–1584. DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/bty862.

  • plmc / EVmutation: Hopf, T. A., Ingraham, J. B., Poelwijk, F. J., et al. (2017). Mutation effects predicted from sequence co-variation. Nature Biotechnology 35(2), 128–135. DOI: 10.1038/nbt.3769.

  • ESM-2 and ESMFold: Lin, Z., Akin, H., Rao, R., et al. (2023). Evolutionary-scale prediction of atomic-level protein structure with a language model. Science 379(6637), 1123–1130. DOI: 10.1126/science.ade2574.

  • Zero-shot variant scoring: Meier, J., Rao, R., Verkuil, R., Liu, J., Sercu, T., and Rives, A. (2021). Language models enable zero-shot prediction of the effects of mutations on protein function. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 34, 29287–29303.

  • BioEmu equilibrium ensembles: Lewis, S., Hempel, T., Jiménez-Luna, J., et al. (2025). Scalable emulation of protein equilibrium ensembles with generative deep learning. Science 389(6761), eadv9817. DOI: 10.1126/science.adv9817.

  • Classic RFdiffusion demonstration: Watson, J. L., Juergens, D., Bennett, N. R., et al. (2023). De novo design of protein structure and function with RFdiffusion. Nature 620(7976), 1089–1100. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8.

  • RFdiffusion3: published update: Butcher, J., Krishna, R., Mitra, R., et al. (2026). Atomic functional site scaffolding in biopolymers with RFdiffusion3. Science, eaeh2982 [First Release, 15 Oct 2026]. DOI: 10.1126/science.aeh2982.

Supporting methods and further reading

  • Foldseek-Multimer and PDB100 construction: Kim, W., Mirdita, M., Levy Karin, E., et al. (2025). Rapid and sensitive protein complex alignment with Foldseek-Multimer. Nature Methods 22(3), 469–472. DOI: 10.1038/s41592-025-02593-7.

  • Generative family models / GREMLIN: Balakrishnan, S., Kamisetty, H., Carbonell, J. G., et al. (2011). Learning generative models for protein fold families. Proteins: Structure, Function, and Bioinformatics 79(4), 1061–1078. DOI: 10.1002/prot.22934.

  • Pseudolikelihood Potts inference: Ekeberg, M., Lövkvist, C., Lan, Y., Weigt, M., and Aurell, E. (2013). Improved contact prediction in proteins: using pseudolikelihoods to infer Potts models. Physical Review E 87(1), 012707. DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevE.87.012707.

  • AlphaFold 3 access-history addendum: Abramson, J., Adler, J., Dunger, J., et al. (2024). Addendum: Accurate structure prediction of biomolecular interactions with AlphaFold 3. Nature 636, E4. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-08416-7.

  • Experimental context for DHFR Asp27: Liu, C. T., Francis, K., Layfield, J. P., et al. (2014). Escherichia coli dihydrofolate reductase catalyzed proton and hydride transfers: temporal order and the roles of Asp27 and Tyr100. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences USA 111(51), 18231–18236. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1415940111.
