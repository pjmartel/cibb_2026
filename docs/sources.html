Reference provenance and reuse information for the lecture and its molecular examples.

Lecture literature

The References page lists the lecture's primary papers and supporting methods, with a downloadable bibliography. Coordinate records and search provenance are documented separately below.

Data and molecular figures

The molecular illustrations are renderings of actual coordinate references, not generated protein shapes. The lecture retains experimental-versus-predicted labels and separates illustrative workflows from computed results.

  • Myoglobin: 1MBN.
  • Leghemoglobin: 1FSL.
  • Calmodulin experimental comparison: 1CLL; prediction and confidence data: AlphaFold model.
  • Barnase–barstar: 1BRS, the displayed biological assembly.
  • Lysozyme–benzene: 4W52.
  • Adenylate kinase: 4AKE and 1AKE.
  • Constrained-design illustration: classic RFdiffusion input example, explicitly a provided target model, not a newly generated binder.

Search provenance and limits

The Foldseek example uses a saved 1MBN-chain-A query against PDB100, with server-reported version 20240101 (server metadata). Identical full-length myoglobin sequences occur in the returned list; the exact reason for their retention in that monomer job has not been established (saved response).

The site and notes therefore do not guarantee one returned hit per unique sequence. The lecture's displayed search and alignment numbers are preserved, rather than replaced by a new live search.

Software, fonts and reuse

External tools remain governed by their own licences and terms. PyMOL is not bundled, and this repository distributes no AI weights or service credentials.

The class visualisation scripts, ligalign.py and struct_diff.py, were supplied by Paulo Martel and are included unchanged. Their downloadable copies and toolkit ZIP preserve the same source bytes; these scripts do not generate the lecture's molecular predictions.

Typography uses Zodiak and Satoshi via Fontshare, with system fallbacks. No blanket reuse licence has been selected for the original course material or helper code; consult the repository's licensing note before redistribution.
