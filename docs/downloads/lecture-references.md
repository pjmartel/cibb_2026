# Tools for structural Bioinformatics: lecture references

Verified 22 October 2026. Author lists use an explicitly abbreviated author–year style; titles link to the checked publication records.

## Prediction and structural comparison

- **AlphaFold 2 and confidence:** Jumper, J., Evans, R., Pritzel, A., et al. (2021). [Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34265844/?dopt=Abstract). Nature 596(7873), 583–589. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2.

- **AlphaFold 3 and biomolecular complexes:** Abramson, J., Adler, J., Dunger, J., et al. (2024). [Accurate structure prediction of biomolecular interactions with AlphaFold 3](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38718835/). Nature 630(8016), 493–500. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-07487-w.

- **Boltz-2: structure and affinity:** Passaro, S., Corso, G., Wohlwend, J., et al. (2025). [Boltz-2: Towards Accurate and Efficient Binding Affinity Prediction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12262699/). bioRxiv, 2025.06.14.659707 [preprint]. DOI: 10.1101/2025.06.14.659707.

- **Foldseek structure search:** van Kempen, M., Kim, S. S., Tumescheit, C., et al. (2024). [Fast and accurate protein structure search with Foldseek](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37156916/). Nature Biotechnology 42(2), 243–246. DOI: 10.1038/s41587-023-01773-0.

- **TM-align structural alignment:** Zhang, Y., and Skolnick, J. (2005). [TM-align: a protein structure alignment algorithm based on the TM-score](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15849316/). Nucleic Acids Research 33(7), 2302–2309. DOI: 10.1093/nar/gki524.

- **T4 lysozyme–benzene: PDB 4W52:** Merski, M., Fischer, M., Balius, T. E., Eidam, O., and Shoichet, B. K. (2015). [Homologous ligands accommodated by discrete conformations of a buried cavity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25847998/). Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences USA 112(16), 5039–5044. DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1500806112.

## Sequence models, ensembles and design

- **EVcouplings framework:** Hopf, T. A., Green, A. G., Schubert, B., et al. (2019). [The EVcouplings Python framework for coevolutionary sequence analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30304492/). Bioinformatics 35(9), 1582–1584. DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/bty862.

- **plmc / EVmutation:** Hopf, T. A., Ingraham, J. B., Poelwijk, F. J., et al. (2017). [Mutation effects predicted from sequence co-variation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28092658/). Nature Biotechnology 35(2), 128–135. DOI: 10.1038/nbt.3769.

- **ESM-2 and ESMFold:** Lin, Z., Akin, H., Rao, R., et al. (2023). [Evolutionary-scale prediction of atomic-level protein structure with a language model](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36927031/). Science 379(6637), 1123–1130. DOI: 10.1126/science.ade2574.

- **Zero-shot variant scoring:** Meier, J., Rao, R., Verkuil, R., Liu, J., Sercu, T., and Rives, A. (2021). [Language models enable zero-shot prediction of the effects of mutations on protein function](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/f51338d736f95dd42427296047067694-Abstract.html). Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 34, 29287–29303.

- **BioEmu equilibrium ensembles:** Lewis, S., Hempel, T., Jiménez-Luna, J., et al. (2025). [Scalable emulation of protein equilibrium ensembles with generative deep learning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40638710/). Science 389(6761), eadv9817. DOI: 10.1126/science.adv9817.

- **Classic RFdiffusion demonstration:** Watson, J. L., Juergens, D., Bennett, N. R., et al. (2023). [De novo design of protein structure and function with RFdiffusion](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06415-8). Nature 620(7976), 1089–1100. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06415-8.

- **RFdiffusion3: published update:** Butcher, J., Krishna, R., Mitra, R., et al. (2026). [Atomic functional site scaffolding in biopolymers with RFdiffusion3](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aeh2982). Science, eaeh2982 [First Release, 15 Oct 2026]. DOI: 10.1126/science.aeh2982.
