# Reuse and licensing

No blanket open-source or Creative Commons licence has been selected for the original course text, slide artwork or helper code. Inclusion in this repository is not an additional licence grant; the course owner should choose any intended redistribution terms before wider reuse.

Third-party data, software, fonts and linked materials remain subject to their respective terms. Molecular reference structures are attributed in `docs/sources.html` and `pymol_scripts/examples/README.md`; the repository does not distribute PyMOL binaries, model weights or external software.

Public visibility is not confidentiality. Do not commit restricted research data or an instructor answer key to a public repository, even outside the deployed `docs/` directory.
